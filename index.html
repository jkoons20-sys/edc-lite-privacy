Privacy Policy — EDC-lite

Effective date: October 4, 2026

EDC-lite is a vehicle diagnostic app for VW/Audi TDI vehicles (EDC15/EDC16 engine controllers). It reads diagnostic data from your vehicle through a USB KKL cable or a Bluetooth OBD-II adapter.

Data we collect

None. EDC-lite does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

  • No accounts. The app has no sign-up, no login, and no user profiles.
  • No analytics. The app contains no analytics SDKs and sends no usage statistics anywhere.
  • No advertising. The app contains no ads and no advertising SDKs.
  • No network access. The app does not access the internet at all.

Vehicle data

Fault codes, live data, and dash readings shown by the app come directly from your vehicle's engine controller over the USB cable or Bluetooth adapter you connected. This data is displayed on your screen only. It is never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with anyone, including the developer.

Device permissions — why the app asks

  • USB host: to communicate with your KKL diagnostic cable.
  • Bluetooth / Bluetooth connect: to communicate with your Bluetooth OBD-II adapter.
  • Storage (older Android versions only): to save diagnostic reports (fault-code PDFs/CSVs) on your device at your request.

Children

EDC-lite is a tool for vehicle owners and mechanics. It is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no data from anyone.
